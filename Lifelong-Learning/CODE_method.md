# What is Tiago Forte's CODE method?


Tiago Forte’s **CODE method** is the foundational framework for his productivity system known as **"Building a Second Brain" (BASB)**. It is a four-step process designed to help people manage the overwhelming amount of information they encounter daily and turn that information into creative output.

CODE stands for **Capture, Organize, Distill, and Express.**

---

### 1. Capture (Keep What Resonates)
The first step is to stop trying to remember everything in your head. Instead, you move information into a "Second Brain" (a digital notes app like Notion, Obsidian, Evernote, or Apple Notes).

*   **The Rule:** Don't capture everything. Only save things that "resonate" with you, surprise you, or seem useful for a future project.
*   **What to capture:** Quotes from books, screenshots, voice memos, website links, or your own random thoughts.
*   **Goal:** To clear your mental space so you can focus on thinking rather than remembering.

### 2. Organize (Save for Actionability)
Most people organize information by **topic** (e.g., "Economics," "Biology," "Marketing"). Forte argues this is a mistake because it’s hard to find things when you actually need them.

*   **The Method:** He suggests organizing by **Actionability** using a system called **PARA**:
    *   **Projects:** Things you are actively working on with a deadline (e.g., "Finish website redesign").
    *   **Areas:** Long-term responsibilities (e.g., "Health," "Finances," "Parenting").
    *   **Resources:** Topics you are interested in but have no immediate deadline (e.g., "Architecture," "Cooking recipes").
    *   **Archives:** Completed projects or things you no longer need.
*   **Goal:** To put information where it will be most useful for your current work.

### 3. Distill (Find the Essence)
Once you’ve captured a long article or a book chapter, you likely won't have time to re-read the whole thing later. Distilling is the process of finding the "oil" in the "crude oil" of information.

*   **The Technique:** Forte calls this **Progressive Summarization**. 
    *   **Layer 1:** The raw note.
    *   **Layer 2:** Bold the most important sentences.
    *   **Layer 3:** Highlight the best parts of the bolded sentences.
    *   **Layer 4:** Write an Executive Summary at the top in your own words.
*   **Goal:** To make a note "discoverable" so that you can understand the main point in 30 seconds or less in the future.

### 4. Express (Show Your Work)
This is the most important step. Information is useless if it just sits in a digital vault. The point of a Second Brain is to produce "output."

*   **The Concept:** Use the notes you’ve captured, organized, and distilled to create something. This could be a blog post, a work presentation, a business plan, or a creative project.
*   **Intermediate Packets:** Instead of trying to sit down and write a whole book at once, you assemble "intermediate packets"—small chunks of work (like an outline, a collection of quotes, or a draft) that you have previously distilled.
*   **Goal:** To shift from being a passive consumer of information to an active creator.

---

### Summary of the Philosophy
The CODE method is built on the idea that **your brain is for having ideas, not for holding them.** By outsourcing the "holding" to a digital system, you free up your biological brain to do what it does best: connect dots, think critically, and be creative.
